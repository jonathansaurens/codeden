- **Members and invites.** A CodeDen server can now be shared: owners invite people from Settings › Members with a link that works once, for 7 days. The invitee picks a display name and a passphrase and is signed in. Owners see everyone's devices and can sign them out, disable or delete members. Existing installs keep working as before: the network access password is the first owner's passphrase, and devices that were signed in stay signed in as the owner.
