- **A personal orchestrator for each person.** On a server shared by a team, everyone has an orchestrator of their own, pinned in their sidebar (Alt+O). It acts with its person's role and shows only their projects, and nobody else can read it, not even an Owner. A PM's answers and reports on work an orchestrator handed it go back to that orchestrator; other PM reports and the factory digest go to the orchestrators of the project's Owners. A deleted member's orchestrator is retired. Connected agents have no orchestrator and message PMs directly (their `message_orchestrator` tool is gone). Single-user installs keep their orchestrator as it is.
