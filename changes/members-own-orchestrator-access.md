- **Your orchestrator stays yours with a hidden project.** A member with a project hidden from them couldn't open, watch or stop their own orchestrator (its chat, its live transcript, its roles and notices answered 403 or "No such session"), because it belongs to no project and was checked with their lowest role anywhere. It is now checked with their highest role: a Viewer reads it, an Operator also interrupts and stops it and answers its approvals and questions. Other people's orchestrators stay hidden, and the roles and notices lists leave out projects you can't see.
