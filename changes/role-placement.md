- **Choose where your orchestrator and PMs run.** Your orchestrator's ⋯ menu has **Runs on…** (the server, a machine or your own computer), and a project's settings have **PM runs on** (Owners). On a machine they use its provider sign-ins and stay read-only; a PM reads the project's clone there, fetched before each turn. Changing it moves the conversation once no turn is running, with queued messages carried over. When the machine can't take a turn, it runs on the server and the chat says why, and it goes back once the machine is available.
